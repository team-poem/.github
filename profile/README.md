# Hello, we are POEM ✍️

**P**rojects **O**ften **E**nd **M**iserably.  
The name means “projects often end miserably.”

It’s true. Projects often fail.  
Some projects never reach the end.  
Other projects reach the end, but we still hope for more.

Still, we keep writing our own poems.

Each repository here is a poem.  
Some poems may fade from memory.  
Other poems may stay with someone for a long time.

Did a poem touch your heart? Please leave a ⭐.  
Your star says, “Someone read your poem.”
